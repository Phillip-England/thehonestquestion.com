01 / AN HONEST PLACE TO BEGIN

You can be honest here.

Maybe you’re holding a question you’re afraid to ask. Maybe things that used to feel simple now feel heavy. Maybe you just need someone to listen.

The Honest Question is a place for Christians navigating fear, doubt, and the in-between. We’re here to make room for thoughtful conversation, at your pace and without pressure to arrive at a neat answer.
